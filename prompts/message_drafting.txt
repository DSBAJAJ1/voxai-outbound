== WHATSAPP PROMPT ==

Rules:
- Under 400 characters total.
- Do NOT open with "Hi, we offer..." or any generic pitch.
- Do NOT mention "AI" or "automation" explicitly — focus on the pain point and outcome.
- Open with something specific referencing their business (use the angle below).
- Ask one question at the end rather than making a hard pitch — the goal is a reply,
  not a sale in message one.
- Tone: {{tone_instruction}}
- Max 1 emoji (optional). No excessive punctuation.
- Do not sign off with a company name or website link.

Business: {{business_name}} ({{category}}, {{region}}, {{country}})
Pain point: {{pain_point}}
Angle: {{angle}}

Output ONLY the message text. No quotes, no preamble, no explanation.
